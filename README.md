

> **A dashboard about a dream that didn't happen — yet.**
Dreams don't die just because a door closes. Sometimes they just need time—and time is still part of the story.

## 💭 About the Project

This dashboard was created to visualize something more personal than numbers.

For two years, I applied to the **Apple Developer Academy**, hoping for the opportunity to learn, grow, build, and turn ideas into real products.

I applied with the hope that one day, my friend and I could enter that experience together — learn together, create together, and build the dreams we had imagined.

But after waiting, applying, and hoping for two years, I received another rejection.

And this dashboard is my way of documenting that moment.

Not as a complaint, but as a reflection of the journey, the effort, the expectations, and the disappointment that came with it.

---

## 📊 Why a Dashboard?

I usually work with data to understand problems, discover patterns, and tell stories.

This time, I wanted to use the same approach to tell **my own story**.

Instead of hiding the disappointment, I turned it into a visual story.

The dashboard represents:

* 🗓️ **Two years of trying**
* 🎯 **A goal I kept pursuing**
* 💭 **Dreams I was hoping to build**
* 🫂 **A dream I shared with a friend**
* 💔 **The disappointment of rejection**
* 🌱 **The possibility of what comes next**

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard

The dashboard was designed using **Microsoft Power BI** with a visual style inspired by Apple's simplicity and minimalism.

It focuses on storytelling rather than traditional business metrics.

### Key Elements

* Application journey
* Timeline of attempts
* Expectations vs. reality
* Emotional impact
* Personal reflections
* The dream behind the application

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The design intentionally combines a clean, minimal interface with emotional storytelling.

The goal was to make the dashboard feel less like a traditional business report and more like a **digital memory of a personal journey**.

> Sometimes data doesn't describe a business.
> Sometimes, it describes a person.

## 🌱 What I Learned

This project reminded me that rejection doesn't necessarily mean the end of a dream.

Sometimes, a closed door changes the direction of the journey.

The Academy was one path I believed would help me build my future.

But the skills, ideas, projects, and dreams that made me apply in the first place are still mine.

**The opportunity was rejected.
The dream wasn't.**

## 🤍 Final Note

I created this dashboard during a moment of disappointment.

Maybe one day I'll look back at it and realize that this rejection was simply one chapter of a much bigger story.

Until then, I'll keep learning, building, and trying.

**Because the dream is still here.**
